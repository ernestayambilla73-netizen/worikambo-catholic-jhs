Administrator guide

  • Use a named, authorised administrator account; do not share passwords.
  • Promote users only after approval by school leadership.
  • Student reports are private school records. Access depends on Supabase row-level security policies.
  • Community posts should be reviewed before approval.
  • If a section reports a database or permission error, check the corresponding Supabase table, RPC and row-level security policy rather than disabling security.

This dashboard does not create an administrator account or bypass role restrictions.
